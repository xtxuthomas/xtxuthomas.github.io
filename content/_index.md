## About me ([ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-9565-9462)) {#heading-about-me}

I study the evolution of binary stars and the compact objects they leave behind. My research interests include **massive binary evolution, X-ray binaries, gravitational waves, and gamma-ray bursts**. I use detailed stellar and binary evolution models, together with population synthesis, to connect individual systems to their wider populations.

I am a Tsung-Dao Lee Postdoctoral Fellow at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, Shanghai Jiao Tong University. I received my PhD in astrophysics from the University of Bonn in 2024, following my bachelor's and master's studies at Nanjing University.

### Education

- **2019–2024:** PhD in Astrophysics, University of Bonn, Germany.
  Thesis: [Comprehensive population synthesis predictions for massive binary stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud (PDF)](theses/phd-thesis.pdf).
- **2016–2019:** MSc in Astrophysics, Nanjing University, China.
  Thesis: [中子星／黑洞X射线双星的形成与演化 (PDF, Chinese)](theses/masters-thesis.pdf).
- **2012–2016:** Bachelor's degree in Astronomy, Nanjing University, China.

### Research experience

- **2025–present:** Tsung-Dao Lee Postdoctoral Fellow at the Tsung-Dao Lee Institute, Shanghai Jiao Tong University.
- **2025:** Visitor at Nanjing University.

## Research {#heading-research}

- **Massive binary populations.** Detailed evolution models and population synthesis for massive binaries in the Small Magellanic Cloud.
- **Compact objects and X-ray binaries.** Black holes with massive-star companions, accreting pulsars, and the evolution of X-ray binaries.
- **Compact-object mergers and gamma-ray bursts.** How binary evolution produces gravitational-wave sources and gamma-ray burst progenitors.
