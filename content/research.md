My work brings together detailed stellar evolution and population synthesis to study binary stars, X-ray sources, and the progenitors of compact-object mergers.

## Massive binary evolution

I compute detailed binary evolution models using MESA and construct grids of models for population synthesis. A central focus is the population of evolved massive binaries in the Small Magellanic Cloud, connecting detailed models with predictions from rapid population synthesis.

- [Detailed binary evolution predictions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23876)
- [Predictions using the rapid binary evolution code ComBinE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23878)
- [PhD thesis: Comprehensive population synthesis predictions for massive binary stars in the Small Magellanic Cloud](https://bonndoc.ulb.uni-bonn.de/xmlui/handle/20.500.11811/11282)

## Compact objects and X-ray binaries

My research includes the X-ray emission of black holes with massive-star companions, spin evolution in Be/X-ray pulsars, and orbital evolution in black hole low-mass X-ray binaries.

- [X-ray emission from BH+O star binaries](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.01395)
- [The bimodal spin-period distribution of Be/X-ray pulsars](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.04707)
- [A circumbinary disk model for rapid orbital shrinkage](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.07914)

## Compact-object mergers

I study binary evolution channels relevant to gravitational-wave sources and energetic transients, including unequal-mass binary black hole mergers and population constraints on neutron star–white dwarf mergers.

- [Population constraints on NS–WD merger gamma-ray bursts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.10551)
- [Unequal-mass binary black hole mergers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.12426)
