## Stellar model grids

I contributed to the **MPA Stellar Model Grid Repository**, including the SMC binary MC simulation2 dataset.

[Browse the Stellar Model Grid Repository](https://wwwmpa.mpa-garching.mpg.de/stellgrid/)

## Methods and related work

My calculations combine detailed MESA binary evolution models with population synthesis. The following works describe the modelling and population predictions:

- [Detailed binary evolution models for the Small Magellanic Cloud](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23876)
- [PhD thesis](https://bonndoc.ulb.uni-bonn.de/xmlui/handle/20.500.11811/11282)
